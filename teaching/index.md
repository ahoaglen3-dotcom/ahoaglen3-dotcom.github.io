# Teaching Experience

## Teaching Philosophy

By providing students with an abundance of primary resources, historical context, and cultural information, their critical thinking abilities and communication skills will vastly expand. I want my students to become informed and educated citizens and to experience enlightenment.

## Valencia College

- Freshman Composition I and II
These gateway courses provide explicit instruction on the writing process, which includes everything from pre-reading and brainstorming, outlining, essay writing, and revision. Composition I focuses on expository essay and rhetorical modes. Composition II utilizes literature, including poetry and short stories, to reinforce the essay writing process, as well as research skills.

- New Student Experience
This course taught first semester students how to navagate the college experience and included topics such as note-taking, study skills, financial planning, and advising for career planning, and registration assistance.  

- Introduction to Humanities 
This gateway course provides a survey of humanities content on the Western cannon that is organized historically from ancient humanity to contemporary culture. 

- Ancient/Greek & Roman, Renaissance & Baroque, and 20th Century Humanities 
These three courses were "Gordon Rule" courses meaning that students were taught writing skills and were expected to write a minimum of 
6000 words over the course of the term. Content areas focused on Western cannon humanities and included the disciplines of art history, phillosophy, religion, performing arts, and literature.

## Southern New Hampshire University

- Self, Society, and Humanities
This first-year survey course teaches students about the way in which humans create art and respond to social experiences. Topics include cultural ideas about food in creative works, performing arts, and cultural appreciation/appropriation. 

- Culture and Creativity
This second-year course evaluates the way in which the creator of art and consumer of art are interdependent and cyclical in nature. Students create a scaffolded project during the term that evaluates one creator and their respective works.
